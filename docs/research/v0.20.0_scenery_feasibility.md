# v0.20.0 – Real-World Scenery Sources & Feasibility

**Research snapshot: 2026-11-21; updated after inspecting the user-supplied GLB on 2026-11-21.** Status: **partially completed; verification remains open**.
This record distinguishes metadata/documentation verification, executable tests and work which still requires downloading or opening third-party files. The initial renderer change provides online **2D raster imagery** for Berlin and Brandenburg; it does **not** provide 3D terrain elevations, 3D buildings, collision meshes or validated flyable interiors.

All use WMS 1.3.0, EPSG:3857, transparent PNG, Cesium imagery rectangles and the official combined attribution `© GeoBasis-DE/LGB, dl-de/by-2-0; © Geoportal Berlin, dl-de/zero-2-0`. Geographic limiting rectangle from the published DOP20c resource: **west 11.1528°, south 51.2635°, east 14.8390°, north 53.6099°**; it includes the Rüdersdorf reference anchor at **52.484197° N, 13.789789° E**. This is a rectangular approximation of **service** coverage, not an assertion that all pixels in the rectangle have current orthophotos. Other map styles share this rectangle; the respective services' exact footprint is not independently checked.

The WMS layers are loaded only online. They **do not** use the Swiss offline cache; custom imagery providers remain authoritative, and diagnostic offline/prepared-offline maps never silently request German WMS tiles. The startup Swiss camera and navigation remain unchanged. New imagery appears on camera navigation to Berlin/Brandenburg or existing coordinate searches. LGB requests and licensing are documented under map attribution and privacy notices. No bulk downloading is implemented.

**Important limitations:** The terrain provider is still the swisstopo quantized-mesh service; no German DEM was converted or installed as a CesiumTerrainProvider. A 1-m **DGM WMS hillshade is a raster visualization**, not the numeric DGM height grid. Site-specific terrain heights and collision/failsafe calculations remain **unvalidated** outside Swiss terrain coverage. Swiss 3D buildings/vegetation services do not become German 3D datasets automatically. The next milestone tasks must add true 3D terrain and suitable buildings, with status/coverage handling and verified physics before advertising full simulation in Germany.

## Candidate data sources

### A. Rüdersdorf photogrammetry – actual GLB inspected (2026-11-21)

- [Zenodo record 10288797](https://zenodo.org/records/10288797), **Abandoned Chemical plant Rüdersdorf – RAWscan**, creator **Andrea Spognetta (spogna)**, source Sketchfab/Objaverse.
- **Actual user-supplied original file inspected locally**: `d2464ec36fb644f28e904e5517f1da64.glb`, **44,199,708 bytes** (42.15 MiB); MD5 **`c687d665d1e0ee68f69477207941d103`**, matching Zenodo's published checksum. Do not include this asset in Git or application bundles.
- GLB 2.0, 1 scene, 17 nodes, **15 meshes / 15 indexed TRIANGLES primitives / exactly 1,141,010 triangles** (from index counts). 932,447 per-primitive vertex entries; **570,074 unique vertex positions** after exact-coordinate welding across meshes (corresponding closely to Sketchfab's rounded 570.1k vertex listing). The difference is attributable to duplicate coordinates across primitives and seams; it is not 932k distinct spatial vertices.
- **1 material**, `KHR_materials_unlit`, `doubleSided: true`; **1 embedded JPEG texture, 1024 × 1024 pixels**, 655,113 compressed bytes; embedded UVs and normals; no Draco compression. A 1024² atlas for the whole site imposes an immediate close-range texture quality limit.
- Native (pre-node-transform) geometry bounds: **X -8.206688 to +8.206688, Y -8.601963 to +8.601963, Z -2.748936 to +2.748936**. Overall extents **16.413376 × 17.203926 × 5.497872 *model units***, **not established metres**. The Sketchfab root node rotates native Z-up geometry into glTF Y-up coordinates. **No embedded geographic coordinates, physical calibration or survey control points**. The known location **52.484197° N, 13.789789° E** is external source metadata, not a verified model anchor or transform.
- **Topological diagnostic**: after *exact* coordinate welding (without simplifying faces), **1,712,219 distinct undirected triangle edges**, **1,452 singly incident boundary edges** and **22 edges incident to more than two faces**. ~**99% of boundary vertices** lie within 0.25 *model units* of the footprint bounding rectangle. This indicates most open mesh boundary is the cropped outer scan perimeter, not an obvious network of empty door/window openings. Welded topology and boundary statistics do **not** prove the existence or absence of traversable openings; closed tunnel-type passages can exist in watertight meshes. Avoid treating the raw scan as collision-ready.
- A local software rasterization of the actual triangle/UV geometry produced top-down and oblique previews: industrial structures, roofs and chimneys are visibly recognizable, **but blurry/fragmented reconstruction areas, distorted details, and texture artifacts are prominent**. No high-quality internally navigable rooms or reliable window openings can be confirmed; do not promise indoor freestyle from the original asset. **Blender was not available**, so this is an offline geometry/render diagnostic rather than an interactive Blender inspection.
- **License resolved**: the [original Sketchfab page](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/abandoned-chemical-plant-rudersdorf-rawscan-d2464ec36fb644f28e904e5517f1da64) expressly states **CC Attribution–NonCommercial (CC BY-NC)**. A locally tested non-commercial proof of concept is distinct from redistributing the asset inside FPVManager, particularly for any current/future commercial distribution. Obtain additional permission from Andrea Spognetta before commercial use; preserve attribution and license terms for permitted non-commercial derivatives. Free availability on Zenodo does not override this restriction.
- **Approximate raw graphics-buffer footprint**: ~43.5 MB of decoded geometry/attribute/index buffers (15 primitives, 32-bit index accessors), plus ~4 MiB uncompressed RGBA texture, excluding GPU/JS copies, padding, runtime allocations and any generated collision mesh. The actual peak memory and rendered FPS have **not** been measured.
- **Cesium integration status:** bundled CesiumJS 1.145 supports local GLB models and WGS84-ENU transforms, and a standalone local-only test page `tool/scenery/cesium_glb_probe.html` was added. **Headless browser navigation to the local test server was blocked by execution-environment policy (`ERR_BLOCKED_BY_ADMINISTRATOR`); no actual WebGL load/render is claimed.** A developer must run the probe manually on Windows/Android with the supplied GLB and tune scale, altitude, heading and anchor against survey reference data.

**Recommended treatment:** do not ship this GLB as the scenery's definitive collision mesh, do not assume 1 local unit = 1 metre, and do not embed it in version control. Use it as an optional local **non-commercial visual feasibility fixture**. Build an accurately scaled/located scenery from suitably licensed geodata, manually reconstructed flyable geometry and independent collision meshes.

### B. Brandenburg LoD2 buildings

- Openly published [Brandenburg 3D Buildings](https://data.geobasis-bb.de/geobasis/daten/3d_gebaeude/) directory; 1×1 km CityGML LoD2 tile archives under [`lod2_gml/`](https://data.geobasis-bb.de/geobasis/daten/3d_gebaeude/lod2_gml/).
- [Official WFS LoD2](https://isk.geobasis-bb.de/ows/gebaeudelod2_wfs?) also exists.
- License: **Datenlizenz Deutschland – Namensnennung 2.0** per LGB, requiring attribution; check per dataset and Berlin contributions separately.
- Generalized roof/wall volumes are useful for geographic anchors and outer collision proxies, **not** detailed open windows, industrial pipework, open hall interiors, real facade textures or sufficiently precise FPV freestyle gaps. Do not conflate LoD2 presence with specific factory coverage. Relevant Rüdersdorf tiles and their contents still need to be checked.

### C. Berlin LoD2 building data

- Berlin publishes [LoD2 CityGML building data](https://daten.berlin.de/) (find the current LoD2 3D building dataset / Geoportal Berlin data download). Its conditions must be checked on the relevant dataset, distinct from Brandenburg LoD2. Berlin and Brandenburg should not be treated as a single 3D-city data source.

### D. Brandenburg LiDAR and digital terrain models

- [LGB LAZ point-cloud downloads](https://data.geobasis-bb.de/geobasis/daten/als/laz/) (source LiDAR, **not** a ready-made Cesium mesh).
- [LGB DGM downloads](https://data.geobasis-bb.de/geobasis/daten/dgm/) (DTM raster/XYZ data); use the **numeric** elevations, not the `dgmshade` WMS image, for terrain reconstruction.
- Elevation products use official height datums. Determine the delivered vertical CRS and convert to WGS84 ellipsoidal heights before combining them with Cesium height queries. Validate LiDAR acquisition dates against modeled building state.
- DGM/LAZ are not automatically sufficient to reconstruct complete indoor surfaces; LiDAR acquisition from above often misses vertical and hidden geometry.
- LGB open geodata directory states **dl-de/by-2-0**. Attribution and transformed-data notices must be retained in the eventual package.

### E. Orthophotos (DOP20)

- [LGB DOP20c official WMS](https://isk.geobasis-bb.de/mapproxy/dop20c/service/wms?request=GetCapabilities&service=WMS); declared **20 cm ground resolution**. Use for alignment and outdoor roof/ground texture references, not precise building heights or indoor geometry.
- Historical vintages may be useful where industrial structures changed; verify the acquisition period before claiming an as-built scene.
- The live WMS is for dynamic view and not a bulk texture download pipeline. For bundled/offline texture products, obtain licensed download tiles/services separately.

### F. OGC 3D Tiles

- [Official 3D-Mesh-Dienst Brandenburg metadata](https://data.gov.de/suche/daten/3d-mesh-dienst-brandenburg) and tileset candidate `https://isk.geobasis-bb.de/geodienste/lgb/3d_sentinel/3dmeshes/tileset.json`, published October 2026.
- Format targets Cesium 3D Tiles with on-demand LOD and has LGB **dl-de/by-2-0** metadata. **The service is not documented as providing full spatial coverage of every site. Rüdersdorf inclusion was not verified in this execution.** Inspect bounding volumes in the actual tileset and try Cesium live before selecting it as the site source.
- Do not treat a 3D Tiles surface as an independent terrain-height provider or fly-through collision geometry without further processing.

## Feasibility and coordinate integration

The currently bundled Cesium 1.145 engine provides `Model.fromGltfAsync`, `Cartesian3.fromDegrees` and `Transforms.eastNorthUpToFixedFrame`. An automated test verifies a numerically valid WGS84 **ENU frame** at the Rüdersdorf anchor. This is an API/coordinate **feasibility check**, **not** a rendered GLB demonstration. The actual model must be inspected, aligned and rendered in a real WebGL browser before the Cesium integration acceptance criterion is checked.

Suggested model import sequence after source rights are confirmed:

1. Use verified GLB metadata (triangles, texture, native axis orientation) from this report, then inspect the mesh interactively in Blender for scale calibration and actual openings.
2. Establish 2–3 independently sourced control points. Convert EPSG:25833 / vertical datum when using official CityGML/DGM.
3. Export local-meter glTF/GLB at a consistent origin. Use Cesium WGS84 ENU matrix at the geographic anchor (not untransformed UTM coordinates).
4. Make separately optimized visual and collision meshes. Explicitly preserve flyable gaps and thin steel structures.
5. Perform a Cesium browser proof of concept using the imported GLB without default building removal; then test base-map overlap and replacement rules.
6. Measure camera performance on Android S22/S25 Ultra and Windows; do not allow visual LOD transitions to change collision geometry.

## Initial performance **targets** (unmeasured; revise after first asset inspection)

| Profile | Initial incremental scenery GPU/CPU asset budget | Display goal | Physics requirement |
|---|---|---|---|
| S22 mobile, compatibility | **up to 96 MiB** combined scenery assets | **30 FPS target** | Existing fixed-step logic and swept collisions unaffected |
| S25 Ultra mobile, balanced | **up to 192 MiB** combined scenery assets | **45–60 FPS target** | No frame-rate-dependent collision simplification |
| Windows, balanced/quality | **up to 384 MiB** combined scenery assets | **60 FPS target** | Separate collision mesh and bounded spatial queries |

These are engineering **proposals**, not validated hardware ceilings. GLB transfer bytes do **not** equal GPU texture + buffer allocations. Collect per-device GPU/JS/Dart memory, texture decode peaks, model load wall time, geometry counts, frame time p50/p95, physics step p95 and terrain collision continuity. Compare to an otherwise identical flight/camera path with scenery disabled.
