**Original model:** Andrea Spognetta (spogna), *Abandoned Chemical plant Rüdersdorf – RAWscan* ([Sketchfab original](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/abandoned-chemical-plant-rudersdorf-rawscan-d2464ec36fb644f28e904e5517f1da64), [Zenodo record](https://zenodo.org/records/10288797)).

Model, original scan textures, derivative geometry and collision meshes retain this separate license; **not** Apache-2.0. Commercial exploitation requires appropriate permission.

FPVManager combines source-derived photogrammetry with bounded, approximate architectural additions, surface repairs and a matching GLB/FPC collision model. New structures are photo-informed estimates, **not measured buildings or flight clearances**. Photographs supplied or consulted as visual references are not redistributed inside the model. A downsized texture atlas may be used for mobile builds.

**Additional sources** (independent attribution and conditions): official BKG 3D-Beta LoD2 buildings and terrain for test-only runtime preview; Brandenburg/LGB orthophotos; official DGM1 height model (DHHN2016); BKG GCG2016 anomaly grid. Use the on-map **Sources** panel, the dataset's source credits and [third-party notices](../../../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md). The BKG beta vertical datum is not independently verified for radio planning or collision safety.
